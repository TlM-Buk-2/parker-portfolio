Assistive Robotics Project

Computer Vision & Lidar Enhanced Cane

A smart cane prototype that combines depth vision, planar lidar, and haptic feedback to help visually impaired users sense nearby obstacles and directional threats.

Oak-D Lite RP Lidar Raspberry Pi 4 Haptic Feedback Computer Vision

Overview

This team project augments a traditional cane with onboard sensing and feedback so users keep familiar tactile sweeping while gaining earlier awareness of obstacles beyond the cane tip. A Raspberry Pi 4 manages sensor input and triggers haptic cues when objects enter critical ranges.

Sensing is split between an Oak-D Lite depth camera for computer-vision object detection and an RP Lidar unit for planar ranging. Feedback is delivered through rumble motors and a servo-powered directional tap that indicates which side a detected threat is approaching from.

Object Detection

  • Oak-D Lite depth camera with onboard processing
  • RP Lidar with ~12 m planar range
  • Wooden cane still usable for traditional tactile feedback

Haptic Feedback

  • Rumble motor vibrates when lidar threshold is breached
  • Servo taps the arm on the side of a CV-detected threat
  • Pi coordinates sensor fusion and feedback timing using ROS2

Prototype Build

  • Repurposed mop-handle shaft with custom handle
  • 3D-printed mounts and hose-clamp fixtures
